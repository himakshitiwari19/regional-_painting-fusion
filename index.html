Vriksha — a Warli–Kalamkari fusion

A Kalamkari tree of life grows and breathes in madder-red and indigo pen-work at the centre, crowned by a slowly turning sun mandala, while rice-white Warli dancers circle its roots and a flock of geometric birds drifts overhead — two regional painting languages animated as one living scene, framed by a pulsing triangle border.
